Fix your form before the next rep

Backsight is a video delay app for iPhone and iPad. It films every rep and plays it back a few seconds later, so you can watch your swing, routine or jump right after you finish it, without touching your phone.

Common questions

What is a video delay app?

It shows what the camera saw a few seconds ago, all the time. Do your rep, look at the screen and watch it, without pressing record or rewinding. Backsight is a video delay app for iPhone and iPad.

How do I watch my golf swing a few seconds later?

Put your iPhone or iPad on a stand where it sees your whole swing, open Backsight and keep the 5-second delay it starts with, or pick your own. Swing, look up, and there it is. Press volume down to save the swing and watch it again in slow motion.

How long can the delay be?

Anywhere from 1 to 60 seconds, with presets for 3, 5, 10, 20 and 30. Lock the delay so a bump never changes it.

Does Backsight do slow motion?

Yes. Save rep replays the last 5 to 60 seconds in slow motion, and saved reps play at quarter, half or full speed, frame by frame if you like. Turn on Smooth slow motion to film at up to 240 frames a second, on cameras that can.

What does the outer screen show on iPhone Duo?

The delayed video and the slow-motion replays, facing the athlete, while the coach keeps the live view and the controls on the big screen. It appears while the Duo is open and Backsight's camera is running.

Can I show the delayed video on a TV?

Yes, with iOS 27 or later. Connect a TV or monitor by cable or AirPlay, and Backsight shows the athlete view on it: the delayed video and the slow-motion replays.

Is there a subscription?

No. Backsight is $9.99 once, and every feature is included. The only in-app purchase is the optional $2.99 Theme Pack, which adds seven color themes and matching app icons. Nothing else is locked.

Which iPhones and iPads does it work on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 18 or later. The athlete screen needs an iPhone Duo, and the TV or monitor view needs iOS 27 or later.

Is my video private?

Yes. There's no account, no ads and no tracking. Your video stays on your device unless you save it to Photos or share it, and Backsight never uses the microphone, so saved reps are silent. The privacy policy has the details.

Can my school buy Backsight?

Yes. Schools can buy it through Apple School Manager, with a discount on 20 or more copies. Every feature comes with the app, so students get all of them.
