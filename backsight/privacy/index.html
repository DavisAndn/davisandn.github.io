Backsight Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

Backsight: Video Delay Replay (“Backsight”) is a camera app that plays your movement back a few seconds later. It works entirely on your device. This policy explains what that means for your data.

The short version

Backsight doesn't collect any data. It has no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or third-party services, and it doesn't send your video anywhere.

Camera and video

  • Backsight uses the camera only to show live and delayed video while you use the app.
  • Recent video is kept in your device's memory so it can be replayed. It's discarded when the session ends or the app closes. It's never uploaded, and it isn't stored unless you save it.
  • A clip leaves the app only when you choose to save it to your photo library or share it. After that, Photos or the app you shared it with handles it.
  • Backsight doesn't use the microphone, so its videos have no sound.
  • If you show video on another screen, such as the outer display of iPhone Duo or a TV over AirPlay, it goes directly to that screen.

Settings

Your preferences, such as the delay length, are stored only on your device and in your device's backups, if you use them.

Information Apple provides

Apple handles purchases, downloads and payments under Apple's own privacy policy. If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may share crash reports and anonymous usage statistics with us. We use them only to fix problems and improve the app.

Children

Coaches and parents often use Backsight to film young athletes. Because the app doesn't collect or send any data, it doesn't collect information from children either. Video of anyone you film stays on your device unless you choose to save or share it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
