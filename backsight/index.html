Backsight: Video Delay Replay

Backsight films each rep and plays it back a few seconds later, so you can watch what you just did without touching your phone.

Common questions

Why does Backsight need the camera?

It films you so it can play the video back. Backsight never asks for the microphone, so its videos are silent.

Where does my video go?

It stays on your device. Recent video is kept in memory so it can be replayed, and it's discarded when the session ends. A clip leaves the app only if you save it to Photos or share it. The privacy policy has the details.

When does the athlete screen appear on iPhone Duo?

When the Duo is open and Backsight is on its camera screen with the camera running. iPhone Duo decides when to show it, and Backsight works fully without it.
